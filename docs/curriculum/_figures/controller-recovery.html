A timeout or restart in any Trial in flight mode leads to Reconciling (row 14). Reconciling can resume the trial (rows 17 and 20) or resolve it back to Ready (rows 15, 16, 18, and 19). A restart in Proposing returns to Ready (row 21). A score for no pending trial is logged and ignored in any mode (row 22). After a stop, late evidence closes the pending trial in Stopped (row 23).
