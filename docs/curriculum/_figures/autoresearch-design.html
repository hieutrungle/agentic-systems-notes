Build context

Give the policy the goal, the available operations, and relevant ledger entries such as the latest score and remaining compute.

Learned policy

Propose trial

Choose a configuration to test a hypothesis or replicate a gain, or propose to stop. Return the proposed arguments, such as learning rate and seed.

Runtime

Authorize trial

Check arguments, permissions, and compute. An approved request can proceed to dispatch. A rejection or pause leaves the job unsubmitted.

Executor and evaluator

Run and score

The executor submits the approved request. The scheduler assigns a job ID, workers train the configuration, and the evaluator returns a validation score.

Runtime

Record result

Update the ledger with the score and cost. Promote a candidate that strictly beats the incumbent and passes the protocol checks.

Runtime

Check limits

Apply the trial cap and compute limit, and accept or decline a proposed stop. Continue by building context from the updated ledger, or stop and freeze the incumbent.

Fixed evaluator

Evaluate final holdout

Score the frozen selection on reserved data. This score assesses the final choice and stays outside the search loop.
