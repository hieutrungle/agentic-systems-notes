Select a mode or group, then one of its rows.

Ready sits at the top of the Search group. Under limits, row 1 leads to Proposing. The policy owns two edges from Proposing: trial proposed (row 3) leads to Authorizing, and stop proposed (row 4) returns to Ready. Allowed (row 7) leads into the Trial in flight group: Dispatching, Running, and Scoring, linked by accepted (row 8) and job done (row 9). Rejected, job failed, scored, invalid output, and resolved return to Ready along a line on the right. Row 2 leads from Ready to Stopped, row 12 leads from anywhere in Search to Stopped, and row 13 leads from Stopped to Done.

Mode

Stopped

The search is over. No edge leads back to a new trial.

Mode

Done

Terminal mode. No row leaves Done.

Group

Search

Every mode inside the large group, from Ready to Reconciling.

None

Mode

Ready

The only mode that starts a policy turn. The stop rule runs here every time.

Event
Limits checked (controller)
Guard

Rows 1 and 2 share a mode and an event. Their guards exclude each other, so exactly one fires.

Mode

Proposing

The policy's turn. Both policy-owned edges leave from here.

Row 3 · Proposing → Authorizing

Fired by: Policy

Event
Trial proposed (policy)
Guard
The reply answers the current turn
Update
Count the proposal and record its arguments

One of the policy's two choices. A stale reply from an earlier turn fails the guard and matches no row.

Row 4 · Proposing → Ready

Fired by: Policy

Event
Stop proposed (policy)
Guard
The reply answers the current turn
Update
Count the proposal and record the stop request

A stop proposal is a request. Row 2 acts on it in Ready. The policy can ask to stop early, but it cannot keep the run going past a limit.

Row 5 · Proposing → Ready

Fired by: Decoder

Event
Invalid output (decoder)
Guard
The report concerns the current turn
Update
Count the proposal and record the error for the next context

A reply the decoder cannot use, or one that misses its deadline, still counts against the 10-proposal cap.

Mode

Row 7 · Authorizing → Dispatching

Fired by: Gate

Allowed (gate)
Guard
The approval binds the recorded arguments, the estimate fits the unreserved compute, and no stop is requested
Update

Commit before sending. A crash after the commit leaves evidence that the request may have been sent. The text below explains why.

Group

Trial in flight

A timeout or restart in any of these modes goes to Reconciling. Rows 14 and 15 handle it, with the second trace in §7.

Mode

Scoring

The job finished, and a score is requested.

Row 11 · Scoring → Ready

Fired by: Evaluator

Event
Score (evaluator)
Guard
The score is for the pending trial and comes from evaluator v1
Update
Charge the cost, keep or discard the candidate, and close the trial

Promotion happens only here and in row 16. The policy cannot produce a score event, so it cannot promote a candidate.

Mode

Reconciling

Reconciling resolves timeouts, restarts, and late replies. Rows 14–20 come with the second trace in §7.
